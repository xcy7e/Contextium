# Contextium Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 29 August 2026

Contextium (package name: `app.xcy7e.contextium`) respects your privacy.

## Data collection

Contextium does not collect, transmit, share, sell, or otherwise process personal data.

The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting services, or third-party data collection SDKs.

## Local data storage

Data created in Contextium, such as context menu entries and their configuration, is stored locally on your device.

This data is not transmitted to the developer or to any third party. It remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

## Network access

Contextium does not transmit data to any server operated by the developer.

Links or URLs configured by you may open in another app or browser. The privacy practices of the respective website or service then apply.

## Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL and the date above will be adjusted.
